# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is prospective freelance clients looking for social media and design services. Agencies and employers evaluating Alexandra for professional opportunities are a secondary audience.

Visitors need to quickly understand Alexandra's capabilities, assess the quality and results of her work, and decide whether to contact her.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Alexandra Barbosa's experience, services, and selected client work. Its primary purpose is to generate quote requests and win new freelance clients. If freelance conversion is limited, it should also support relevant employment opportunities.

Success means that qualified visitors understand the combined social media and design offer, trust the evidence in the project work, and make contact about a concrete opportunity.

## Positioning

Alexandra combines social media strategy and management with hands-on design, content creation, copywriting, and a distinctly human understanding of internet culture. The portfolio should communicate that clients can work with one creative partner across both strategic thinking and execution.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients and employers evaluate the portfolio by reviewing Alexandra's profile, services, experience, selected projects, project outcomes, downloadable CV, and external professional profiles. They can start a conversation through the displayed email address or the contact form, which opens a pre-filled email.

The website must be available in both Portuguese and English, with a clear way to switch between the two languages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Present Alexandra's profile, experience, services, client history, and portfolio projects.
- Provide individual project pages with factual descriptions, responsibilities, credits, assets, and results where available.
- Support direct contact for quote requests and professional opportunities.
- Offer the complete visitor experience in Portuguese and English.
- Keep project claims, metrics, dates, clients, and collaborator credits accurate to the evidence on hand; future work must not invent proof or outcomes.
- Preserve the combined focus on social media and design rather than presenting either discipline in isolation.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and identity: Alexandra Barbosa / `alexadinis`.
- Voice: playful, warm, direct, conversational, and recognizably human while remaining credible to prospective clients.
- Core offer: social media and design, spanning strategy and creative execution.

## Evidence on Hand

- Nine portfolio project records are currently defined in `src/data/projects.ts`, with varying levels of detail.
- Detailed work and assets are available for Endesa Portugal, KFC Portugal, Munchie, Feel Better, and L&R Ópticas.
- Current project evidence includes results such as Endesa's reported increase in impressions and reach, KFC's reported Instagram engagement and TikTok follower growth, dates, agency attribution, and collaborator credits.
- Professional experience is recorded in `src/components/About/ExperienceSlider.tsx`.
- A downloadable CV is available at `public/alexandra-barbosa-cv.pdf`.
- A profile portrait and project imagery are available under `public/`.
- LinkedIn, Behance, Instagram, and direct email contact are linked from the website.
- Some projects contain only summary-level information or placeholder imagery; future work must not fabricate missing case-study details.

## Product Principles

1. Convert attention into qualified freelance enquiries before optimizing for general exposure.
2. Prove capability through real work, clear responsibilities, and truthful outcomes.
3. Present social media strategy and design as one connected, end-to-end offer.
4. Keep every interaction and piece of copy playful, personal, and easy to understand.
5. Give Portuguese- and English-speaking visitors an equally complete experience.
